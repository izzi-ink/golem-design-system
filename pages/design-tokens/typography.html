Usage Guidelines

  • Light (300)
    Subtle text, large displays
  • Normal (400)
    Body text, paragraphs
  • Medium (500)
    Buttons, labels
  • Semibold (600)
    Headings, emphasis
  • Bold (700)
    Strong emphasis, titles

AI Integration

🤖

Semantic Typography for AI

Golem's typography tokens help AI understand the purpose of text, not just its size:

✅ AI-Friendly Names

  • text-display-large - Page titles
  • text-headline-medium - Section headings
  • text-body-medium - Regular content
  • text-body-small - Supporting text

❌ Abstract Names

  • text-5xl - How big is it?
  • text-2xl - When to use?
  • text-base - What's the purpose?
  • text-sm - Why small?
